# docs: the logd article reads as slogans; write it in the register of docs/tony.md

`docs/articles/logd-document-store.md` is written in a compressed, punchy register: sentence fragments ("Two files."), bold slogans, verdict lines, first person singular, and an em dash in most paragraphs. `docs/tony.md` is the project's reference voice: full declarative sentences, a term defined where it first appears, comparisons made as "Like X ... Unlike X ...", an explanation for the reader who does not know the background, and limits stated plainly.

Rewrite the article's prose in that voice. The facts, the examples, the numbers and the project's terms stay as they are; the section numbers go, since tony.md has none, and cross-references name the section instead.
