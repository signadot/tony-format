Review of 94a1a20c found the same defect after a quoted field: the closing quote ends the segment, but both field branches of `parseKFrag` recursed straight into `parseKFrag`, so `a.'x'*` read as `a.x.*`, `a.'x'y` as `a.x.y` and `'x'y` as `x.y`. Fixed in 46683504: both field branches continue with `parseAfterSegment`. An unquoted field runs up to `.`, `[`, `{` or `(`, so it is unaffected (`a.b*` is still the field `b*`). The renderer emits none of the refused forms.
