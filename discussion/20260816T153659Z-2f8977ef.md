Fixed in 1b3621b (go-tony/v0.0.148). mergeop finds an arraydiff element's op the way SplitChild does -- the first label the registry knows, not simply the first -- and the labels ahead of it go back on the value, which is what lets a delete compare a braced object against the braced element it deletes. logd's marker moved to the tail of the chain (tx.MarkPatchRoot), where it cannot be read as a label of the value. Regression tests in mergeop/arraydiff_tagmask_test.go and system/logd/storage/arraywrite_test.go, both confirmed failing without the fix.
