PROPERTY 6, STATED RATHER THAN PROPOSED, and prerequisite 5 with it. It is the one thing on
this issue that was drafted without ever being given a number, so this comment gives it one.

  6. THE INDEX IS BOUNDED ON RESIDENCY, NOT ON CONTENT. Above the line. The index describes
     all of history, durably; what it HOLDS is chosen by a policy over (commit, path) under a
     ceiling that is configured rather than emergent. The invariant that makes any policy safe
     is EVICTION CHANGES A COST, NEVER AN ANSWER.

Why it is a property and not a tuning note: the five in the description bound the TRANSIENT --
a read's working set against the answer's size -- and the measured store's problem is the
FLOOR, 1.12 GB of index resident out of a 1.31 GB live floor. A read that is out of memory by
construction on top of an O(history) resident index has bounded the wrong term. Property 5 is
complementary and not a substitute: compaction shrinks what there is to describe (4,519,816 ->
47,648), residency bounds what is held of it.

The work it costs is not the policy, which is why LRU is enough to start. It is the inversion
underneath: today the index IS the truth and index.gob a copy kept for restart, and eviction is
correct only the other way round. That is what makes prerequisite 5 the same kind of thing as
the other four -- a signature-level decision that cannot be taken one caller at a time.

Prerequisite 5 is index_residency.md (f57e769, re-attached with this comment). It now opens by
naming itself, as the other four do.
