## Refinement to component L — format negotiation is 1-hop (edge only)

Supersedes the "pass it through end-to-end and to controllers" phrasing in #005.

Format is an edge/presentation concern, not an internal-fabric concern. It terminates
at the client-facing hop; behind that, everything speaks wire.

- client <-> docd: format negotiated (client may request wire/json/yaml).
- docd <-> controller: wire.
- docd <-> logd: wire.
- controller <-> logd: wire.

docd transcodes at its own client edge if a client requests json/yaml, and uses wire
for all internal hops (to controllers and to logd). Same rule for logd's own client
edge: a client talking directly to logd negotiates format on that 1 hop, then wire
internally.

Net: only the outermost client-facing hop honors EncodingOptions; docd/logd do not
propagate a client's requested format inward. Avoids threading an encoding option
through every internal message.
