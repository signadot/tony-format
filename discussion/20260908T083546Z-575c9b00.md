A SNAPSHOT IS OF A PATH. Its entry carries the path, its segment sits there and nowhere else, and a read seeks to the nearest snapshot at or above its path with the greatest commit, opened at the read's path within it. When one is taken is decided by the reads: a read that folded more than 64 records at a path, of a subtree under 1 MiB, schedules a snapshot of that path as of the commit it read, off the reader, one at a time, serialized with the switch. A write's own read of its site is a read, so a hot written path stays snapshotted and its verification read stays short. Reads that did not finish, absent paths, commits behind the root snapshot, and subtrees over the budget decline. Retention treats it as a write: kept within the cutoff, dropped beyond, no tier slot. Counters: reads.seek.path, reads.folded, reads.tail.max, snapshots.path. The storage suite runs 20s faster under the default policy -- the differentials' own folds got shorter.

COMPACTION WALKS THE FILE IT REWRITES (decision 8). Its work list was the root's segments, and a segment indexed only at its path was never in it -- found the day it mattered: a snapshot of a path survived a cutoff-0 compaction at a stale position, the below-root-copies defect again. The inactive log's own records are now the work list; a dropped entry's segments are derived from it and removed before the rewrite, a survivor's re-derived and moved after (index.EachSegment, the walk that indexed it). What compaction holds is one record per entry of the file and one entry at a time, not every segment of both logs.

A SCOPE'S ENTRY GOES WHEN A LATER ONE DOMINATES IT (5hmq80f3). Sound because stored scope writes are absolute; needs no composition and materializes nothing. The cover rules were written from the fold's answers, probed first: a later plain write keeps the earlier node's head comment, an array replaces up to its own length and merges object elements, an empty object merges nothing away. So !raw and !delete cover totally; a plain scalar or array of plain scalars covers everything beneath and, at its own path, only an untagged uncommented statement; the rest cover nothing. A dropped scope entry raises the store-wide replay floor -- pessimistic, and only for a resume from beyond the cutoff. Differential against a never-compacting store: both views, every path, 150 seeds.
