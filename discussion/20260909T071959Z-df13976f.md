-m Answered by the logd rebuild (wk5w1ddkh12krj1tkxn0), phase 6 (c23ad3f) -- and the answer is that the bound is on RESIDENCY, not on size. An index truncated to live state would be small and would make history unusably slow by construction; an event store whose index stops at the present has stopped being one. So the index still describes everything, and what it HOLDS is bounded.

The durable index is now the truth (index.regions plus a manifest) and the resident trie is a cache of it, in regions of (node, commit range). Only a durable, clean region is evicted, so eviction changes a cost and never an answer. LRU, a configured ceiling with a floor, and the counters to see it: index.resident.bytes, index.ceiling, index.hit.rate, index.evictions, index.over.bytes. Measured against the same index unbounded and reopened from its files, and against an unbounded store at every path and commit.

The measurement this issue asked for is in index_residency.md: 1.12 GB resident on staging, 85% of the live floor, ~266 bytes of B-tree leaf per segment across 4,519,816 segments.
