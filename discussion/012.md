## .meta extended + docd<->logd reconnect decided (21ad7a9, e927082)

.meta surface (21ad7a9): added .meta (resource index) and .meta/schema (per-mount
schema contributions; composition still future). Stored schema nodes are cloned
per response so concurrent reads are race-safe. CLI: `o sys docd schema` beside
`o sys docd mounts`.

docd<->logd reconnect (e927082): decided DIAL BACKOFF ONLY. docd retries its logd
dial with exponential backoff (startup ordering / brief unavailability) but does
NOT reimplement mid-session reconnect/watch-replay. Rationale (scott): the client
already has that "transparency dance" in LogdSession (reconnect + watch replay
from last commit), and it works identically against docd or logd, so duplicating
it in docd would just be a second copy to drift. On a mid-session logd drop docd
tears the session down and the client's LogdSession reconnects and replays. docd
stays a thin, fail-fast proxy.

Note: this relied on a protocol fact — the logd session protocol has no
mid-stream "this watch died" signal, so a watch only fails when its connection
closes. That is why keeping a session alive across a logd drop would require
transparent watch replay; pushing it to the client sidesteps that entirely.
