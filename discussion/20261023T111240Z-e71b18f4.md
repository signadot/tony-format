From the review of 21c70fc7: the size-inferred detection has a second gap on the persister's side too, in principle. Rewrite's trailing Persist appends the regions dirtied during the copy to the new file before it writes the manifest; a crash after those appends and before the manifest is detected only while the new file is still shorter than the old manifest's FileSize, which needs the appends to stay under the garbage just dropped. On a store of any size that holds; on a small index at startup it need not. Removing the manifest before the rename, as proposed above, covers both windows exactly instead of by size.
