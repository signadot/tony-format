# two answers to this issue's question are in the tree, pointing different ways

This issue had no links of any kind until now -- not even to eagjggjdh12ksg00bsn0, which its
own text names as a dependency. Relating the cluster (above) is most of what was missing; this
comment is the part the links cannot say.

## the plan says: allow anything, store the lowered Diff

`go-tony/system/logd/storage/docs/scope_overlay_plan.md`, direction set by fdf4bdd:

> A patch may be written with arbitrary expressivity; what the log stores is
> `Diff(before, after)`, lowered to a vocabulary in which THE DATA REFLECTS THE RESULT -- an
> operation leaves no residual meaning once applied. [...] the restriction belongs on storage
> rather than being a SCOPE-SPECIFIC CONCESSION.

It is further along than a direction. §7 resolves the exact form -- "a lowered delta is the
WRITE EXPRESSED ABSOLUTELY, not the minimal difference: Diff(before, after), then union in an
assertion at each path the patch named, valued from after" -- measured across update, add,
coincident write and delete in `keyed_lowering_writes_test.go`, which is in the tree. §6 is
this issue's phase 2 under that scheme: selectSurvivors stops exempting scope patches, behind a
per-scope replay floor and a read-set divergence check. And fdf4bdd notes it DISSOLVES §3.2
rather than answering it: if nothing relative is stored, there is nothing to freeze.

## what shipped says: a scope may not write a relative op at all

6679423, six days later, closing 3xn08cb6h12kr4psg5n0: ValidateForStorage on scoped write data,
refusing !replace, !arraydiff, !strdiff, !rename, !jsonpatch, !if and !let in a scope. That is
the scope-specific concession fdf4bdd rejected by name.

It was not framed as a reversal -- 3xn08cb6's closing note offers it as support for THIS issue:
"there is no relative op in a scope for an absolute materialization to stand in for. That
premise is now ENFORCED rather than believed." Which is true, and is also how the conflict
became invisible: the restriction reads as a strengthening if you are looking at this issue,
and as a rejection if you are looking at the plan.

## why it matters before anyone builds this

The two lead to different work. Under the plan, the keyed half of this issue's approach --
"build a bounded materialized overlay from the latest patch per leaf-path" -- is largely
unnecessary, because a stored delta is already absolute and already carries !key(f) in its own
tag (§3.5, P1). Under what shipped, that construction is the whole job, and the vocabulary
restriction stays a permanent property of scopes.

They also disagree about what a scope IS. The plan makes a scope an ordinary writer whose
deltas are lowered like everyone's; the shipped rule makes a scope a second-class writer with a
smaller vocabulary, which is what docs/logd/writes.md now documents to users.

## what is downstream of the answer

- 9b2vpggxh12ks0qde5n0 and sb33w8p9h12kr16kg5n0 are both about scoped materialization cost, and
