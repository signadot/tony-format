Widened in the fix. The parser had the same gap in objects: `{a: 1,,b: 2}` read as `{a: 1, b: 2}` while `[1,,2]` was refused. Both now refuse a ',' with no element before it, in objects as in arrays: the parser in token.balanceBrObj and the decoder in readEvent. docs/tony.md's Commas section lists the invalid object forms beside the array ones.
