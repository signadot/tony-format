-m PHASE 3b LANDED: 31d3e27. The head is a number. Prerequisite 1 is now whole -- read side (8dab04f) and write side -- and no whole document is held anywhere on the read or write path.

A write is verified and lowered at each SITE it states something at: one bounded read of the value there under the write budget (128 MiB, storage.writeBudget), the write's node folded onto it, the diff taken there where lowering is needed, the site deltas marked where they land and rooted by MergePatches. A precondition is StateAt(path) against the already-lowered pattern. Ten writes to ten leaves of a staging-shaped store read the root zero times.

THE WRITE BUDGET, as decided: a !replace of a subtree past 128 MiB is refused naming the path, the operation and the budget, and reaches the client as its own mistake (invalid_diff). A plain merge at a leaf is never refused for size. This is the one client-visible change of the phase.

FOUND on the way: a write carrying a head comment on a CONTAINER is a statement at that container. ClaimPaths walks through the comment to the leaf -- right for a scope's claim, since claiming the container would freeze it -- but a baseline diff taken at the leaf never sees the comment and the log lost it, on 24 of 25 seeds of the scope differential. LowerSites is ClaimPaths that stops at a commented node.

Also: the marker on a lowered delta now lands at the site rather than at the container the whole-document diff descended to (more selective; one row of TestLoweredMarkerLandsOnTheChange records where a scope stores as sent under the client's marker); the head's tests went with the head, and lower_comment / lower_matrix compare a watcher's fold with the read instead of the head with a replay.

NOTED FOR LATER, per Scott: !all and its kind will someday want a PATCH EXPLODER -- one write of !all at a container becomes one write per element, each within the budget, committed as one transaction. It is the natural continuation of per-path lowering and the answer to the case the budget refuses. Recorded at WriteBudgetError and as decision 9 in rebuild_plan.md. Not built.

WORTH WATCHING: the storage package's suite went from ~47s to ~88s. Every write now reads its sites through the cursor, and the differential tests make thousands of writes; the per-write cost is a bounded read where it was a pointer copy off the head. Proportionate on real traffic (a leaf), and the price of not holding the document; but it is where phase 7's per-path snapshots and, later, a warm path for the site read would show.
