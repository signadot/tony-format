Fixed in ef000e9c, as asked: the `noInit` watch is ended the way the floor refusal ends one.

- **logd.** A `noInit` watch with an absolute cursor whose state is no longer exact is confirmed, then ended with `replay_compacted` from `forwardEvents`, by `failWatch`, beside the floor check. libctl surfaces it as a `WatchEndedError` with `Reason == replay_compacted`. verse's `Since` needs no change.
- **docd.** A composed watch in that case is ended the same way, confirmed and then a terminal `replay_compacted` event, before any sub-watch starts. docd decides it from the ping's floor: an absolute `noInit` cursor below it cannot start as asked, whether its deltas are gone or only its state is.

Also in that commit: the error-code forwarding b293631a added to docd for a refused logd sub-watch is removed. It was for the request-error form.
