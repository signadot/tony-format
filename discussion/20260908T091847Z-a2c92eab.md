-m PHASE 6 LANDED: c23ad3f. Index residency, the inversion first.

THE DURABLE INDEX IS THE TRUTH. index.regions (append-only, a record per region) and index.manifest (which records are current, with every region's header) replace index.gob; a store opens with the skeleton and every header and pages nothing to do so; a missing, torn, other-version or generation-mismatched pair rebuilds from the log, saying why. The resident trie is a cache: the skeleton is the floor, each node's segments sit in regions of at most 64 contiguous in StartCommit, the unit admitted, counted, touched and evicted.

THE INVARIANT holds by what may be evicted: only a durable, clean region -- one whose record holds exactly what the tree does. Hot regions stay until Persist writes them, and Persist writes only what the file lacks. A read pages what its range needs and answers in the same critical section that installs it; a write inserts in that section too. Headers answer the newest commit, the seek's target and the watermarks without paging. LRU, a configured ceiling (indexCeiling), a floor of eight regions refused below, and the counters in the report: index.resident.bytes, index.ceiling, index.hits, index.misses, index.hit.rate, index.evictions, index.over.bytes.

TESTED as the document says: one index under a floor-sized ceiling against itself unbounded and reopened from its files, every question; a store under that ceiling against an unbounded one at every path and commit, with evictions and misses counted; the shaped store served under a tenth of its index. Full logd green.

FOUND underneath, and older than this phase: a B-tree emptied to its last element kept an interior root with no children and silently dropped every later insert, and removing a child's last element answered false. Nothing had emptied a tree before; eviction does it routinely. Also: a write into a cold region livelocked with its own page-in under a floor-sized ceiling until the insert moved inside the paging.

NOT DECIDED, deliberately: frecency (the seam is the region and the list); whether the skeleton is itself evictable (it is what UnwrittenBelow proves absence from); a regions-file rewrite on a garbage threshold rather than at close.

WHAT REMAINS OF THE PLAN: phase 5, the wire -- presence on the wire, one rooting rule, protocol 2, the response encoded from the event stream -- which needs verse's four files and libctl to move in lockstep with a go-tony release, so it is the one for you to schedule.
