!raw merges as data: rawOp.Patch hands its child to the ordinary walk under a context that
says everything beneath is data (OpContext.AsData), so an object merges field by field, an
array by position, a scalar replaces, and nothing beneath is interpreted. The match side is
unchanged and exact, and matchpatch.md says both halves. "Exactly this, as data" is
!insert.raw, which a diff already emitted; a scope's lowered claim is that chain now
(claimValue), and under the cover rules it is a total cover of its path, which is what the
footprint work needed from this. libdiff.Escape, with no caller, is gone. The issue's three
lines are TestRawPatchMergesAsData, under a head comment on either side too.

The migration was verse's, as the issue's cost section anticipated: entity.Raw and AsData
compose !insert.raw for a write and keep the bare !raw for a pattern, since a match cannot
hold !insert (verse 75395cec); the escaped records are re-put once and live scopes deleted
at the upgrade. One thing fell out of the escape entering the merge for the first time: a
merged container dropped the line comment on its key, which a verbatim !raw had never had
the chance to do -- 040x4f26h12kr5acm5n0, fixed in 523944b.
