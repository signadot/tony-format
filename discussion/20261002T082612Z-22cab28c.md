Decided (Scott): the watch **starts from the ceiling**. It is not refused. This replaces the "Rule" section above.

A watch from `F`, at or above the replay floor, whose state at `F` cannot be read exactly:

- starts at `X`, the commit a read at `F` is answered at (cpqj2tf6h12kr5jxqxn0: the oldest root snapshot at or after `F`, or the head);
- sends its state at `X`, labelled `X`, and replays the deltas after `X`;
- says so in the watch result: `replayingFrom` is `X`.

The deltas between `F` and `X` are not sent, even where they are retained: they cannot be applied to a state at `X`.

**With `noInit` and an absolute cursor**, there is no state event to move the client to `X`, and a client holding the state at `F` would miss the deltas up to `X`. That watch is refused with `replay_compacted`. A relative cursor with `noInit` claims no position and starts at `X`.

A cursor below the replay floor is refused as before. The ping's `floor` stays the replay floor.

Through docd, a composed watch reads its initial state first when it has a cursor, and starts every sub-watch from the commit that read was answered at.
