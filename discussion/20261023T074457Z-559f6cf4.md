Scott: `!logd-compact(<duration>)`, with `0` meaning what `!logd-current` meant above. Taking that as the shape; what it settles, and what the duration adds to the contract:

```tony
define:
  iod:
    name: string
    out: !logd-compact(0) {delivered: int, failed: int, lastPass: string, lastError: string}
  schedule:
    tick: !logd-compact(1h) {...}
```

- **The argument is the path's own cutoff.** Within it the path's history is exact, as the store's cutoff makes every path's; beyond it a dominated entry goes. `0` keeps nothing past the current value. The duration is written as compaction's are (`1h`, `30m`, `1d`).
- **It shortens, never lengthens.** Beyond the store's cutoff a declared path's records go as every path's do; an argument longer than the store's cutoff changes nothing, and the schema cannot know the store's config, so it is not refused — it is simply the store's cutoff that applies.
- **`0` is zero, not "default".** The compaction config treats an absent or zero field as its default (`compaction.md`, "Zero does not mean zero"); a tag argument is explicit, and `0` here means no history. A `!logd-compact` with no argument is refused at the schema commit: the writer says how much, or does not declare.
- **Dominance is still required at every duration, including `0`.** It is tempting to drop a declared path's old entries by time alone, since the root snapshot the switch writes holds the state and every patch in the inactive log precedes it. That is sound for a read at the head and unsound for a watch: a watcher resuming from a commit before the path's newest write, with that write dropped, starts from an older value and no surviving delta brings it to the current one — silent loss at the declared path, which the floors exist to make loud. Keeping the newest statement of every declared leaf is what dominance guarantees, so an entry goes only when every statement it makes is at a declared path, dominated by a later statement, and older than that path's duration.
- **The floors do not move** for such a drop, at any duration. That is unchanged from the comment above and is the whole point: the exactness given up is the declared path's alone, beyond its own cutoff, and the rest of the store keeps its window.
- **The walk** (option 2 above) carries one more check per candidate: dominated, and older than the path's duration, by the entry's timestamp against `now`, as the store's cutoff is applied.
