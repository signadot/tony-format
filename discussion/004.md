Resolution (commit 9af5a1d): #1 resolved by DECLARATION rather than building graceful in-place membership updates. Event-preservation is a logd guarantee (single commit sequence, FromCommit replays exact history); docd inherits it for single-route watches but declares it BEST-EFFORT across mount boundaries. A composed watch's sub-streams have independent commit sequences, so a membership change ends the watch and the re-watch RE-INITS with a fresh composed snapshot (re-sync to net state) — which a snapshot-diffing consumer (e.g. WatchSubtree) reconciles with no gap. Pinned by a regression test; stated in WatchEndedError + startComposedWatch docs. This keeps 'event-preserving' a clean sentence at the logd layer with the docd asterisk scoped precisely to 'across a mount membership change.'
