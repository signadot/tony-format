Direction changed (Scott, 2026-10-23): the per-path compaction cutoff (`!logd-compact`) is withdrawn, with its three open forks. In its place, the simplest thing that answers this issue:

**Scratch**: one persistent document per store beside the log, with no history. No commits, no compaction, no retention, no schema, no scopes, no watches, no transactions. Two session operations, in their own namespace, not in the document:

```
{scratchWrite: {path: "verse.iods.x.out", data: {...}}}  -> {scratchWrite: {}}
{scratchRead:  {path: "verse.iods.x.out"}}                -> {scratchRead: {data: <node>}}
```

Write is a merge patch (`!delete`, `!replace` etc. as anywhere). Read answers the subtree as it stands now, or null. Persisted whole in `scratch.tony` by write-temp-and-rename, so an acknowledged write survives a crash and the file is never partial. docd routes the two operations to logd as it routes session-level operations: a controller never sees them, and a mount, which claims a document subtree, cannot hold scratch.

For the consumer: `verse.iods.*.out` moves to scratch. Ticks, `~status`, `lastError` stay in the document where your 10:04/10:23 comments want them read at their causing commits. Scratch has no watch: whatever reads `out` on change is driven by the tick it already watches, or polls. Please say whether anything watches `out` today.
