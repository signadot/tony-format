Reopened: b293631a refuses the `noInit` + absolute-cursor watch in a different shape from the floor refusal, and a client that handles the one does not handle the other.

- The floor refusal ends the watch: `failWatch(watcher, replay_compacted, …)` (logd/server/session_watch.go:316 at b293631a). libctl surfaces it on the closed channel as a `WatchEndedError` with `Reason == replay_compacted`.
- The new refusal is a request error: `sendError(id, replay_compacted, …)` (:162). libctl returns it from `Watch()` itself, as `watch error: replay_compacted: …` (libctl/watch.go:75).

The comment above :162 says it "is refused the same way". It is the same code, but not the same delivery.

**Where it lands in verse.** `LogdStore.Since` watches with `NoInit` and an absolute `FromCommit`. It maps only the watch-ended form to `ErrReplayTooOld` (`replayEnded`, entity/logdstore.go:2579); an error from `Watch()` is wrapped plainly. Precondition: a cursor in the window between the floor and the first root snapshot after it, about a cutoff old.
- The engine's catch-up treats the error as transient: the cursor stands, and the next pass is refused again (trigger/engine.go:658). In practice unreachable, since the engine's cursor starts at the head and stays minutes old.
- `verse watch --since <cursor in that window>` answers 503, store unavailable, where a floor refusal answers 409 and names the way out.

**What closes it.** Refuse it as the floor refusal is refused, by ending the watch with `replay_compacted`, so a client has one shape to handle. verse then needs no change.

**Also noted, not a defect.** The ping's `floor` changed meaning here: it is now `watchFloor()`, `AnsweredCommit(ReplayFloor())`. The decision comment above said "the ping's floor stays the replay floor". It has no impact on verse, which reads no floor from a ping, and relative cursors clamp to it consistently in logd and docd.
