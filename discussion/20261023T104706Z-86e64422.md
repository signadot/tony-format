Third draft of the plan (commit b1cc2eb5 on `issue-r482sz5rh12ksj`), after a second review against the code. What it changed:

- Flushes are single-flight: one flush lock from the copy to the rename, so a tick in flight and Close, or two flushes from any cause, cannot write one temp file between them or land out of order. Close waits for a flush in flight before its own.
- Error codes: an apply error is `invalid_diff` (a dense index into a scalar is refused by the apply, as in the document), a read's shape error is `path_conflict`, the catch-all is `storage_error`; there is no `internal` code. A scratch read is never `not_found`: absent is an omitted `data`, stated as a deliberate difference from the document's read.
- The flusher is started unconditionally in `server.New`; every other storage setter sits under "if there is a storage block", and following that pattern would give a store without the block a scratch flushed only at Close.
- A failed apply re-clones the document: `PatchOwned` may have re-parented children into the discarded result.
- The root path is addressable: `!delete` at `""` empties scratch; a result that is null stores as empty, so scratch is never a bare null.
- Close reports a failed final flush after closing the log, not instead of it.
- Compatibility: an older logd or docd refuses the unknown field at decode with `invalid_message`, which is the right answer.
