-m Prerequisite 1 drafted: go-tony/system/logd/storage/docs/read_write_interface.md (bc47800).

read.go says a read varies on VIEW, EXTENT, SOURCE. Two are not the caller's. EXTENT: there is no whole read to be its other value; Read(at, view, "") is a root cursor, not another function. SOURCE: replayed-or-stepped is where to seek from, answered from the index, and no signature lets a caller answer it wrong -- which is the three perf bugs gone by construction rather than by care. VIEW stays a parameter.

Surface: Read, Deltas, Begin/Write/Require/Commit, plus Rooted and Collect over a cursor.

A read is seek, project-and-compose, fold. Resident: one record plus the composed delta, bounded by what changed under kp since the snapshot. The 166 MB and 511 MB terms are both signatures that cannot express that -- []LogSegment becomes a segment cursor, patchNodesFromSegments becomes composition.

Consequence worth flagging: this makes the dependency on property 5 hard. Step 1 is bounded only if there is something bounded to seek TO, and all 246 snapshots are at the root. The interface can be right and the store still slow. 1 and 5 cannot be sequenced apart.

Also: the head is a number, so CheckHead is retired rather than replaced -- with no second computation of the state there is nothing to drift. Collect is the only materialization and takes a budget with no default.
