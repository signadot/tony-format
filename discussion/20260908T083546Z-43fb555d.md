Not an overlay and not a materialized snapshot -- neither turned out to be needed. Since a stored scope write is absolute (the lowering of phase 3b), a scope's entry beyond the cutoff can simply GO when a later entry of the same scope dominates it: states every path it stated, or an ancestor, in a way that does not depend on what was there. The fold of what remains is the fold of everything. The cover rules (what a later !raw, !delete, plain scalar, array or operation says about what was beneath it) are in storage/scope_compaction.go, written from the fold's own behaviour. A hundred rewrites of one field leave one entry; the differential against a never-compacting store holds both views at every path, 150 seeds.

Remaining from this issue's list, deliberately: a per-scope replay floor. A dropped scope entry raises the store-wide floor, so a baseline watcher resuming from before it re-initializes -- pessimistic, and only for a resume from beyond the cutoff.
