Step 1 landed (b39eca8, one storableDelta). Steps 2 and 3 were overtaken: the keyed fallback
went with the rebuild's phase 2, where the schema became the authority and scope_keyed.go
was deleted; and the gate this issue wanted removed so that a scoped write could be lowered
"like any other" is answered the other way by 3e527ec -- a scope stores the CLAIM it made,
not the difference -- and phase P of the scope plan made that claim !insert.raw (d7e5e65).

That is the reason to close rather than continue. A claim is a total cover of its path under
the cover rules the footprint runs on: a later claim retires every earlier statement beneath
it, which is what makes a scoped read and a scoped write flat in the scope's history. A
difference is a merge object, which covers nothing at the object; storing it would have
given the scope exactly the shape dominance cannot bound. So the two pipelines stay two on
purpose, and the asymmetry the issue's doc comment described is the design: baseline stores
what changed, a scope stores what it holds.
