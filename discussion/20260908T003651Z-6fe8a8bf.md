The hard part is not the policy. Today the index IS the truth and index.gob is a copy kept for restart. Eviction is only correct if the durable form is the truth and the resident form a cache of it -- that inversion is the work, and the invariant it buys is what makes any policy safe: EVICTION CHANGES A COST, NEVER AN ANSWER. A read that misses pages in; it never sees a shorter segment list or a path that is not there.

Unit is a REGION, (trie node, commit range), not a node -- a path's recent tail is what reads want and its depths are what they do not, so evicting whole nodes evicts the wrong thing. A region also carries both axes, so swapping LRU for frecency later changes an ordering and not what it operates on.

Enforced at Admit, the one place a region becomes resident, not by a sweep -- a sweep runs after the memory is already held. Resident bytes, ceiling, hit rate, evictions reported: 1.12 GB was found by reading a heap profile after the fact.
