-m Correcting the previous comment: there is no property 6 in the issue -- I was proposing one -- and the shape I proposed was wrong.

"Bounded by the live store, not its history" is a bound on CONTENT. It would be small and would make history unusably slow by construction, which is the opposite defect, not an improvement. An event store whose index stops at the present has stopped being one.

The bound belongs on RESIDENCY. The index describes all of history, durably; what it HOLDS is chosen by a policy over (commit, path) under a configured ceiling rather than an emergent 1.12 GB. Frecency is the obvious shape and both axes are skewed -- path measured (ten shallow paths carry 30.1% of 4.5M segments), commit assumed and worth measuring before anything is tuned to it.

Compaction is complementary, not a substitute: property 5 shrinks what there is to describe (94.9x), residency bounds what is held of it. Together: no I/O at the head, some deep in history, stated as policy instead of arrived at.

This also narrows the earlier "durable btree nodes, but I doubt it" -- durable as the BACKING for evicted regions is not durable as the seek path, so the doubt (every seek becomes I/O) applies to the wholesale version and not to this one.
